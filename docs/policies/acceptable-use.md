# Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-29. Template._

This policy applies to every user of the MDRender Cloud Push service: slave
servers, accounts, and admins. Because file content is end-to-end encrypted and
the operator cannot inspect it, this policy is enforced on **observable
behaviour and credible reports**.

## Prohibited

- Unlawful content, including material that is illegal in the operator's or the
  recipient's jurisdiction.
- Malware, phishing, fraud, or unsolicited bulk messaging.
- Attempting to circumvent bans, rate limits, quotas, or authentication.
- Abusing the relay: ring spam, token enumeration, or excessive retries.
- Impersonating another server, account, or the operator.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, the operator may:

1. Rate-limit or throttle an account, slave, or network source.
2. Suspend (deactivate) an account or slave server.
3. **Ban** an account/email, or a network source by **IP, CIDR range, AS
   number**, hostname, or **domain** (including free and temporary email
   domains).
4. Delete registrations and queued messages.

## Reporting abuse

Report suspected abuse to the operator with the affected server, account, and
time window. Given the server-blind design, actionable reports should include
what was received and why it is abusive. The operator cannot decrypt content to
investigate and will act on metadata, patterns, and reports.

## Appeals

Suspended or banned parties may contact the operator to appeal. The operator's
decision on enforcement is final.
