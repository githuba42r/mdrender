Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026. What this server collects, why, and what happens to it — for MDRender Cloud Push (the “Service”), operated by this server’s operator (the “operator”).

1. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and a salted hash of your password.
  • Devices — names and models you give them, push tokens, pairing and encryption keys, and activity timestamps.
  • Content you send — messages and files pushed to your devices, including anything waiting to be delivered. Pushes to an encrypted device are end-to-end encrypted and unreadable to the operator.
  • Billing records — orders, top-ups, credit movements, and PayPal transaction identifiers, amounts and dates.
  • Technical logs — IP addresses and request logs, used for security, ban enforcement and diagnosing faults.
  • A session cookie — to keep you signed in.

2. How it is used

  • to run the Service: delivering pushes, storing what is pending, and showing your devices, pushes and history;
  • to manage your account and answer support requests;
  • to bill correctly: credit balances, metered usage and payment records;
  • to keep the Service safe: detecting abuse, enforcing bans, and investigating faults.

3. Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal under PayPal’s own privacy policy. This server receives only the payment outcome, amount, date and a transaction reference — card numbers never reach this server.

4. Sharing

Personal data is not sold. It is shared only with PayPal to process payments, with a federated peer server if the operator has federation enabled (limited server metadata: identifier, hostname, public key and last-seen time), and where the operator is legally required to disclose it.

5. Retention

Data is kept while your account is active. Undelivered pushes are purged after they are delivered or expire. Billing records are kept as long as needed for accounting. When an account is deleted, its data is removed within a reasonable period, except records the operator must keep by law.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored as hashes. Where a device is encrypted, pushes and files are end-to-end encrypted so the operator cannot read them. No system is perfectly secure, and the operator cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You can manage your details from the account pages, revoke any paired device at any time, and ask the operator to delete your account. You may also request a copy of the personal data held about you.

8. Cookies

The Service uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in. There are no third-party advertising or tracking cookies on this console.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date at the top shows when it last changed. Questions about privacy, or requests about your data, go to the operator of this server through the support contact published for this deployment.
